WhatsApp Cloud API Setup Guide

Follow this step-by-step guide to connect your WhatsApp Business to ChatIQ. The process takes approximately 10–15 minutes.

Prerequisites

What You Need Before Starting

Ensure you have these ready before beginning the setup process.

Facebook Personal Account

A personal Facebook account to access Meta's Business and Developer platforms.

Meta Business Account

Free to create at business.facebook.com. Required for WhatsApp API access.

WhatsApp Business Number

A phone number registered with WhatsApp Business. Can be your existing number migrated.

Internet Connection

Stable internet access to navigate the Meta Developer dashboard.

Step-by-Step

Connection Process

Follow these 7 steps to connect your WhatsApp Business to ChatIQ.

1

Create a Meta Business Account

If you do not already have a Meta Business Account, go to business.facebook.com and create one. This is a free account that lets you manage your business assets including WhatsApp.

  • Visit business.facebook.com and click "Create Account"
  • Enter your business name, email address, and other details
  • Verify your email and complete the setup
2

Create a Meta Developer App

Go to the Meta Developer Portal (developers.facebook.com) and create a new app. This app will serve as the container for your WhatsApp API integration.

  • Go to developers.facebook.com and click "My Apps"
  • Click "Create App" and select "Business" as the app type
  • Give your app a name (e.g., "ChatIQ Integration")
  • Select your Meta Business Account and click "Create App"
3

Enable WhatsApp Cloud API

Within your Developer App, add the WhatsApp Cloud API product. This will connect your app to WhatsApp's messaging infrastructure.

  • In your app dashboard, click "Add Product"
  • Find "WhatsApp Cloud API" and click "Set Up"
  • Select your Meta Business Account when prompted
  • Register your WhatsApp Business phone number
  • Verify your business phone number with the code sent via SMS or call
4

Generate Your Access Credentials

In the WhatsApp Cloud API settings, you will find three critical pieces of information that you need to copy.

Access Token EAAC... (long string) Click "Copy" in dashboard
Phone Number ID 123456789012345 Numeric ID
WABA ID 987654321098765 WhatsApp Business Account ID

Keep these credentials secure. They are used to authenticate ChatIQ with your WhatsApp account.

5

Paste Credentials into ChatIQ

Log in to your ChatIQ account and navigate to Settings > WhatsApp Integration. Paste the three credentials you copied from the Meta Developer dashboard.

  • Log in to ChatIQ and go to Settings
  • Click on WhatsApp Integration
  • Paste the Access Token in the first field
  • Paste the Phone Number ID in the second field
  • Paste the WABA ID in the third field
6

Save Configuration

Click the Save Configuration button in ChatIQ. The platform will verify your credentials and establish a connection with the WhatsApp Cloud API.

  • Click Save Configuration
  • Wait for the verification process (usually takes a few seconds)
  • You will see a Connected confirmation message
  • Your WhatsApp messages will begin syncing to ChatIQ
7

Start Sending and Receiving Messages

Your ChatIQ is now connected to WhatsApp. You can start managing customer conversations from the shared inbox, set up automations, and run campaigns.

  • Go to the Inbox to view your WhatsApp conversations
  • Invite team members to collaborate on messages
  • Create message templates for common responses
  • Set up automation workflows for routine tasks

Credentials Explained

Understanding Your WhatsApp API Credentials

Learn what each credential does and where to find it in the Meta Developer Dashboard.

Access Token

A long alphanumeric string that authorizes ChatIQ to send and receive messages on behalf of your WhatsApp Business account. It acts as a secure password for API access.

Where to find: WhatsApp > Getting Started > Access Token

Phone Number ID

A unique numeric identifier assigned to your WhatsApp Business phone number. The API uses this ID to route messages to and from your specific number.

Where to find: WhatsApp > Getting Started > Phone Number ID

WABA ID

The WhatsApp Business Account ID is a unique identifier for your overall business account on WhatsApp Cloud API. It links all your phone numbers and message templates.

Where to find: WhatsApp > Getting Started > WABA ID

Quick Tip: Setting Up a Test Number

You can use the test phone number provided by Meta in the Developer Dashboard to test the integration before using your real business number. This is useful for exploring ChatIQ's features without affecting your live conversations.

The test number will appear in the Getting Started section of your WhatsApp Cloud API settings. You can use it alongside your actual business number during the testing phase.
