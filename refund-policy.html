Refund Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

At ChatIQ, we want you to be completely satisfied with our service. This Refund Policy outlines the conditions under which refunds are provided.

1. Free Trial

All plans come with a 14-day free trial. You can explore all features without any charge during this period. No credit card is required to start your trial.

2. Monthly Subscriptions

For monthly subscription plans:

  • You can cancel your subscription at any time
  • Upon cancellation, you retain access until the end of the current billing period
  • No partial refunds are given for the remaining days in a billing period
  • You will not be charged for subsequent billing periods after cancellation

3. Yearly Subscriptions

For yearly subscription plans:

  • You can cancel your yearly subscription at any time
  • Refunds for yearly plans are provided on a pro-rata basis for the unused portion
  • A processing fee may be deducted from the refund amount
  • Refund requests must be made within 30 days of the billing date

4. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be processed within 7-10 business days. Refunds are issued to the original payment method used during purchase.

5. Exceptions

Refunds may be denied in cases of:

  • Violation of our Terms and Conditions
  • Fraudulent activity or abuse of the refund policy
  • Requests made more than 30 days after the billing date (for yearly plans)

6. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact our support team:

  • Email: hello@chatiq.in
  • Include your account email and reason for the refund request

7. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this refund policy at any time. Changes will be communicated via email or platform notification.
