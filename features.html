Features

Everything You Need to Run Your Business on WhatsApp

From shared inbox to AI-powered responses, ChatIQ provides every tool to manage customer conversations at scale.

Shared Team Inbox

Multiple team members can view, assign, and reply to WhatsApp messages from a single shared inbox. Never miss a customer message again.

Broadcast Campaigns

Send personalized bulk messages to thousands of customers. Segment your audience for targeted campaigns with rich media support.

Contact Management

Import, organize, and tag your contacts with custom fields. Keep detailed profiles with conversation history, purchase data, and notes.

Workflow Automation

Build powerful automations with triggers, conditions, and actions. Auto-reply, assign conversations, and send follow-ups automatically.

Customer Segmentation

Segment your audience by behavior, purchase history, location, and custom attributes. Target the right customers every time.

Team Collaboration

Assign conversations, leave internal notes, and track team performance. Keep your support and sales teams aligned.

Analytics Dashboard

Track response times, conversation volumes, campaign performance, and team productivity with real-time dashboards.

AI-Powered Responses

Leverage AI to suggest replies, auto-categorize messages, detect sentiment, and generate intelligent responses.

Message Templates

Create and save reusable message templates for greetings, order updates, payment reminders, and support responses.

Lead Management

Track leads from first message to closed deal. Set pipeline stages and never let a hot lead go cold with automated follow-ups.
