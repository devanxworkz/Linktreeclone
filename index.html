FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ChatIQ. Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out to our support team.

How does ChatIQ integrate with WhatsApp?

ChatIQ connects directly with the official WhatsApp Business API. You can use your existing WhatsApp Business number or apply for a new one through our platform. The integration takes less than 5 minutes and all your existing chats sync automatically.

Is there a free trial? Do I need a credit card?

Yes, we offer a 14-day free trial on all plans with full feature access. No credit card is required to start your trial. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime during the trial period with zero obligations.

How is my data secured on ChatIQ?

We take security seriously. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We are GDPR compliant, host on AWS India servers, and conduct regular third-party security audits. Your customer conversations and contact data are never shared with third parties.

Can my entire team use ChatIQ simultaneously?

Absolutely. Each plan comes with a specific number of team member seats. All team members can access the shared inbox simultaneously, respond to conversations, and collaborate in real-time. You can assign different roles and permissions to each team member.

How long does it take to set up?

Most businesses are fully set up within 10-15 minutes. Create your account, connect your WhatsApp Business number, import your contacts, and you're ready to go. We provide step-by-step onboarding guides and dedicated support for setup assistance.

What kind of support do you offer?

All plans include email support with a 4-hour response time. Growth plan includes live chat support. Pro plan includes priority support with 1-hour response time and a dedicated account manager. Enterprise customers get 24/7 phone support and a custom SLA.

Can I send broadcast messages to my customers?

Yes. Growth and Pro plans include WhatsApp Broadcast Campaigns. You can send personalized bulk messages to segmented customer lists with images, documents, and links. All broadcasts comply with WhatsApp's template messaging policies to ensure high deliverability.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. There are no long-term contracts or early termination fees. If you cancel, you'll retain access to your account until the end of your current billing period. Your data is available for export for 30 days after cancellation.

Does ChatIQ work with international WhatsApp numbers?

Yes, ChatIQ works with WhatsApp Business numbers worldwide. The platform supports all country codes and languages. Our messaging infrastructure is globally distributed to ensure low latency for your customers regardless of their location.

Can I integrate ChatIQ with my existing CRM or tools?

Yes. Pro plan includes API access and custom integrations. We offer native integrations with popular CRMs like HubSpot, Zoho, and Salesforce. We also support webhooks and Zapier integration to connect with 3,000+ apps. Our team can help build custom integrations for enterprise customers.
