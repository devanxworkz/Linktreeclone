About ChatIQ

We are building the simplest, most powerful WhatsApp CRM platform for Indian businesses.

Our Mission

ChatIQ was founded with a simple belief — every business deserves a powerful, affordable tool to manage customer conversations on WhatsApp. We saw businesses struggling with personal phones, missed messages, and scattered spreadsheets. So we built ChatIQ: a platform that brings the power of enterprise CRM to every business in India.
